TravelWise

The name "TravelWise" was chosen because it communicates the site's core promise: helping travelers make smarter, better-informed decisions before and during a trip. It's short, easy to remember, and pairs well with a simple compass or suitcase-style logo mark.

Optional domain availability: travelwise.app

Site Purpose

TravelWise gives travelers a single place to prepare for a trip: current weather at their destination, live currency conversion for budgeting, and practical packing and travel tips organized by destination. The goal is to reduce the number of tabs and apps a traveler needs open before they leave home.

Scenarios

  • A traveler packing for a trip next week wants to know: "What's the weather going to be like at my destination, and should I pack for rain?"
  • A traveler budgeting for a trip abroad wants to know: "How much is 500 US dollars worth in the local currency, and can I convert other amounts quickly?"
  • A first-time international traveler wants to know: "What should I pack for a country with a very different climate than mine?"
