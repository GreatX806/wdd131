Website Planning Document

Cascade Rafting Co.

Site Name

Cascade Rafting Co.

This name was chosen because it directly communicates what the business does (rafting) while "Cascade" evokes the rush of whitewater and ties the brand to the Cascade, Idaho region where the company operates. It is short, memorable, and easy to say aloud when recommending the company to friends.

Optional domain availability: cascaderafting.com

Site Purpose

The site gives prospective and returning guests everything they need to book a trip with confidence: who the company is, what trips are offered, how to reach the team with questions, and a simple way to get in touch about reservations or general inquiries.

Scenarios

  • A first-time rafter wants to know: "Is this trip safe for someone who has never been on a raft before, and what class of rapids will I be on?"
  • A visitor planning a family trip wants to know: "What is the minimum age for kids to join a rafting trip, and are life jackets provided?"
  • A returning guest wants to know: "How do I contact the company directly to ask about rescheduling my reservation?"
